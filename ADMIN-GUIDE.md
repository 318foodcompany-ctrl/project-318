# 318 Food Co. administrator guide

## Sign in safely

- Use the production login only for production work.
- Never share accounts or paste access tokens into chat, email, documents, or source files.
- Sign out on shared devices. If your administrator role changes, sign out and back in to refresh the secure session.

## Daily workflow

1. Open the Dashboard and review new leads, follow-ups, upcoming events, and overdue balances.
2. Move leads through New Lead, Contacted, Proposal Sent, Waiting on Customer, Booked, Completed, or Lost.
3. Set priority, expected revenue, follow-up date, staff assignment, and a clear lost reason.
4. Add private comments without placing sensitive payment data in notes.
5. Create proposals from the linked quote or booking. Verify every line, discount, tax, expiration date, and total before sending.
6. Use the Booking Calendar’s month, week, or day view; add kitchen and delivery times.
7. Create invoices and record payments only from confirmed information. Reverse mistakes—never delete accounting history.

## Customer portal

- Create a portal link only for the correct customer.
- Send links through a trusted channel. Links are credentials and should not be posted publicly.
- Revoke and replace a link if it is forwarded or exposed.
- Portal documents accept PDF, JPG, PNG, or WebP files up to 4 MB.
- Customers can see only issued proposals, non-draft invoices, their events/payments, safe documents, and their conversation.

## Follow-up automation

- Rules begin disabled.
- Review subject, body, trigger, delay, audience, and consent requirements before enabling.
- Booked/completed/lost opportunities stop inappropriate open-lead reminders.
- Promotional lost-lead and review workflows require recorded marketing consent.
- If messages fail or duplicate, disable the rule and scheduler and follow `DISASTER-RECOVERY.md`.

## Customer records

- Search before creating a customer.
- Archive customers instead of deleting them.
- Merge duplicates only after verifying both records. The retained customer receives related history and the duplicate is archived.
- Exported CSV files contain personal information. Store and share them securely, then delete them when no longer needed.

## Website management

- Preview text, menus, photos, testimonials, gallery, FAQs, and event content before publishing.
- Use descriptive alt text that explains the image’s purpose.
- Do not upload copyrighted, confidential, or customer-identifying media without permission.

## When something fails

- Read the on-screen message; do not repeatedly click Save or Send.
- Refresh once and retry only if the message says persistence was not confirmed.
- Record the page, time, action, and visible error—never include passwords or tokens.
- For outages, data concerns, duplicate messages, or unauthorized access, stop and use the disaster-recovery guide.
